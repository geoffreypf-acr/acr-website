Porsche 911 PCCM Plus — 996 and 997

Apple CarPlay, navigation and DAB+ in a water-cooled 911 without spoiling the interior. £1,799.99 supplied and fitted, at your home or office.

August 2026ACR Automobile

Porsche PCCM Plus › Porsche 911

The 996 and 997 are the two 911s people actually use, and the two most let down by their original head unit. A car that will happily cross France on a Sunday still expects you to burn a CD. ACR Automobile fits Porsche Classic’s PCCM Plus to both generations for £1,799.99 supplied and fitted, mobile across London and Surrey.

What your 911 came with

A 996 typically left the factory with a Becker CDR-220 or a Porsche CDR-23 or CDR-24 — a single-slot CD radio in a double-DIN aperture — or, on a well-optioned car, PCM 1 with its small monochrome display and separate CD changer. Early 997s carried the CDR-24 or PCM 2.1. All of them share the same problem: no CarPlay, Bluetooth that either does not exist or handles one paired phone badly, and navigation that needs a CD in the boot to know where Chelsea is.

What the 996 does have, and the reason this job is worth doing properly, is an interior that has aged into being period rather than merely old. Values have turned: a tidy 996 is no longer the cheap 911, and a generic touchscreen cut into the console is now something a buyer marks you down for.

Why PCCM Plus suits a 996 or 997

PCCM Plus is made by Porsche Classic, not by an accessory brand. The 7-inch screen sits in the original double-DIN aperture behind a fascia styled for cars of this age, with real rotary controls either side rather than a bezel of gloss black. In a 996 interior it reads as a factory option you happened to specify. That is the whole point of paying more than an aftermarket unit costs.

  • Apple CarPlay — maps, music and messages through the car’s speakers and steering-wheel controls.
  • Built-in navigation with 3D maps, which works with no phone and no signal — genuinely useful on a car you take away for weekends.
  • DAB+ digital radio, which on a car of this age is a bigger day-to-day improvement than owners expect.
  • Bluetooth hands-free and streaming for any phone, Android included.
  • Reversing camera input — worth having on a 997 with the rear wing up, or any 911 you park in a London street.

Fitting it to a 911

The unit goes into the existing aperture on the car’s own wiring. Nothing is cut, no trim is disturbed, and your original CDR or PCM comes back to you boxed — which on a 911 matters more than on most cars, because the next owner will ask. We fit at your address, set up CarPlay with your phone, tune DAB, and check the steering-wheel controls and any factory amplifier still behave before we leave.

One thing to confirm before we come: later 997.2 cars (2009 onwards) shipped with PCM 3.0 or 3.1, which is a different unit in a different fascia. Send a photo of your centre console and we will tell you exactly which Porsche Classic unit your car takes — PCCM Plus, single-DIN PCCM, or a CarPlay retrofit into the PCM you already have.

Price

£1,799.99 supplied and fitted — the genuine Porsche Classic unit, the correct harness for your car, installation and setup. Not a from-price. A reversing or dash camera and a speaker upgrade are the two things most 911 owners add at the same visit, and both cost less done together than as a return trip.

Frequently asked

Does PCCM Plus fit both the 996 and the 997?
Yes for the 996 throughout, and for the 997.1. Later 997.2 cars from 2009 came with PCM 3.0 or 3.1 in a different fascia, so those need checking — send a photo of your console and we will confirm which Porsche Classic unit fits, or whether a CarPlay retrofit into your existing PCM is the better route.
Will it look out of place in a 996 interior?
No — that is the reason it exists. The fascia is styled for cars of this age, with physical rotary controls rather than a gloss touch panel, so with the screen off the console still reads as original.
Do I lose my steering-wheel controls?
No. We connect them to the new unit and check them before leaving, along with any factory amplifier.
Is it reversible if I sell the car?
Yes. It uses the existing aperture and the car’s own wiring, with no cutting, and your original head unit is returned to you so the car can be put back to standard.
Can you fit it at my home in London?
Yes. We are fully mobile across London and Surrey and fit at your home or office. Most 911s are a single visit of a couple of hours.
