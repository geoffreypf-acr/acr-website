Can a Bentley get Apple CarPlay?

Yes — most Bentleys can have Apple CarPlay added, and the method depends on your model and year. Newer models with the right infotainment system can often have CarPlay activated through coding, while earlier Continental GT, Flying Spur and Bentayga models usually need a retrofit interface module that plugs into the existing screen. We confirm the exact route for your VIN before any work begins.

Direct answer: Bentleys can receive Apple CarPlay in one of three ways — factory activation/coding on compatible systems, a plug-in retrofit module that adds CarPlay to the original screen, or (rarely) a replacement display. ACR selects the method that keeps your factory look intact, then fits it at your home or workplace across London and Surrey.

How does ACR add CarPlay to a Bentley?

There are three possible routes, and the correct one depends entirely on your model, year and current infotainment hardware. We identify this from your registration and VIN before booking, so you know what to expect.

  • Factory activation / coding — where the hardware already supports CarPlay but the feature is dormant, we can enable it in software. This is the cleanest option and keeps everything fully native.
  • Retrofit interface module — a discreet module integrated behind the dash that brings CarPlay to your existing Bentley screen, controlled through the factory display and rotary controller/touchscreen.
  • Replacement screen — only considered where no other route exists, and always discussed with you first.

Wired or wireless Apple CarPlay for a Bentley?

Both are possible depending on the solution fitted. Wired CarPlay connects via the USB port and is the most stable; wireless CarPlay connects automatically over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi once your iPhone is in the car, with no cable needed. Many retrofit modules offer wireless as standard — we'll tell you which applies to your Bentley.

What works after the upgrade — and what stays the same?

Once CarPlay is running, you get Apple Maps and Google/Waze, music and podcasts, calls and messages read aloud, and full Siri voice control — all shown on your Bentley's own display. Everything else stays exactly as Bentley designed it.

Works with CarPlay: navigation, streaming audio, phone calls, WhatsApp/iMessage read-out, Siri, and compatible third-party apps.

Unchanged: your factory dials, climate controls, reversing camera, steering-wheel buttons and original menus remain fully functional. CarPlay sits alongside them, not on top of them.

Does it keep the factory look and my warranty?

Yes. Our goal is an upgrade that looks and feels factory — no aftermarket screens bolted on, no visible wiring. We use OEM-integrated solutions and reversible installation wherever possible. A quality retrofit does not touch your engine, drivetrain or core vehicle systems, so it should not affect your manufacturer warranty on those components. If you have any concern, we're happy to discuss it before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add wireless Apple CarPlay to my Bentley Continental GT?

In most cases, yes. Earlier Continental GT models usually take a retrofit interface module that can support wireless CarPlay through the factory screen. We confirm compatibility from your VIN before booking and quote the exact solution.

Will adding CarPlay affect my Bentley's warranty?

A quality, correctly-integrated CarPlay upgrade does not touch your engine, drivetrain or core vehicle systems, so it should not affect your manufacturer warranty on those components. We use OEM-integrated, reversible methods wherever possible and are happy to discuss any concerns first.

Does the upgrade keep my Bentley's original screen and controls?

Yes. CarPlay runs on your Bentley's existing display and is controlled through the factory touchscreen or rotary controller. Your dials, climate controls, reversing camera and steering-wheel buttons all continue to work exactly as before.
