For businesses

Trade rates for automotive businesses

If vehicles pass through your hands every week, your customers already ask you who they should trust with security. Rather than sending them away to find an installer, ACR Automobile works with automotive businesses across London, Surrey and the Home Counties on trade terms — so you can offer insurance-approved tracking and immobilisation as part of what you already do.

We are Thatcham-accredited and a Meta Trak approved installer. Every installation is carried out by a manufacturer-trained engineer, and insurance certification is issued on the day so your customer can go straight to their insurer. You never handle stock, warranty or paperwork.

Because all of our fitting is mobile, we come to you. Vehicles stay on your premises and your preparation or handover schedule is not disrupted — which matters most to dealers working to a delivery date and to storage facilities where cars should not be moved.

For customers

Refer a friend and earn £50–£75

If we have fitted a tracker or immobiliser to your vehicle and you recommend us to someone else, we pay you for it. There is nothing to sign up to and no code to remember — send us their name and mobile number and we take it from there.

The reward is £50 where the installation comes to less than £1,000, and £75 where it is £1,000 or more. It applies to tracker and immobiliser work only, and it is paid by bank transfer after the job has been completed — never on an enquiry or a booking. There is no cap on how many people you refer.

STEP 01

Send their details

Their name, mobile number and what they drive — by WhatsApp, email or phone. That is your entire involvement.

STEP 02

We look after them

A specialist calls them in your name, carries out a free vehicle security assessment, gives a fixed price and books the fitting.

STEP 03

You get paid

Rewards are paid only after the job has been completed. Once the vehicle is fitted and settled, yours goes out by bank transfer on the next weekly payment run.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you offer trade rates on vehicle trackers?
Yes. We offer trade rates on insurance-approved tracker and immobiliser installation to automotive businesses — prestige and specialist dealers, garages, bodyshops, detailing and paint protection studios, storage facilities, chauffeur companies and fleet operators. Rates are agreed directly with each business, so get in touch and we will talk it through.
How much do I earn for referring someone?
£50 for installations under £1,000, and £75 for installations of £1,000 and above. Referral rewards apply to tracker and immobiliser installations only. There is no limit on how many people you refer.
When is the referral reward paid?
Payment is made only after the job has been completed. Once the vehicle is fitted and the job is settled, your reward goes out by bank transfer on our next weekly payment run.
Does the reward apply to CarPlay or dash cameras?
No. Referral rewards are for tracker and immobiliser installations only. We are always glad of an Apple CarPlay, Android Auto or dash camera recommendation, but there is no reward attached to those.
Which areas do you cover for trade work?
We install across London, Surrey and the Home Counties, working mobile from our West London base in Holland Park, W11. For trade partners we fit at your premises so the vehicle never has to leave your site.
Can you fit trackers at our premises?
Yes. All of our installation is mobile, so we come to your showroom, workshop, storage facility or depot. Vehicles stay on your site and your preparation schedule is not disrupted.
Are the trackers you install insurance approved?
Yes. We are Thatcham-accredited and a Meta Trak approved installer, fitting insurance-approved S5 and S7 trackers, deadlock systems and immobilisers, with certification issued on the day.
Do I need to know anything about trackers to refer someone?
No. Send us a name, a mobile number and the vehicle. We handle the security assessment, the quote, the installation and the aftercare — you are never asked to explain a product.
Will you contact my customers directly?
Only about the vehicle you send us, and always in your name. We do not add them to marketing lists or approach your wider customer base.
