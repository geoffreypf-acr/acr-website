The opportunity

Why dealerships offer security at handover

Vehicle theft is the single thing prestige buyers in London worry about most, and they raise it in the showroom whether or not you bring it up. Right now, most dealers answer it by sending the customer away to find an installer — which loses the margin, loses control of the standard of work, and leaves the customer solving a problem your competitor might have solved for them.

Offering insurance-approved security as part of the handover changes that. It gives the finance office a margin line at a time when income from traditional products has been squeezed, it differentiates you against dealers who do not offer it, and it reduces the post-delivery calls that come when a car is stolen three weeks after it left your forecourt.

A margin line at handover

A security package added at the point of sale replaces income lost elsewhere in the finance office, without adding a product your team has to be trained to sell.

A genuine differentiator

Very few dealers offer Thatcham-accredited security as standard. Being the one that does is a reason for a buyer to choose you over the same car elsewhere.

Fewer theft calls

A car stolen shortly after handover becomes your problem reputationally, whatever the paperwork says. Insurance-approved tracking materially reduces that risk.

A reason to be back in touch

Security renewals and end-of-agreement reminders put you back in front of your own customer at exactly the point they are thinking about their next car.

No stock, no warranty

We supply, fit, certify and support. Your team never holds stock, never handles a warranty claim and never explains a technical specification.

No effect on your schedule

We fit during preparation, on your site. The vehicle does not move, the valet is not disturbed and the delivery date does not slip.

How it works

Built into your existing process

The most common reason dealers stop offering security is that it slows the handover. We designed around that constraint — the work happens before the customer is ever involved.

STEP 01

Book against the prep

You tell us the vehicle and the delivery date. We attend during preparation, alongside the valet and inspection, rather than after the sale is agreed.

STEP 02

Fitted on your site

A manufacturer-trained engineer fits the system at your premises. Concealed, factory-tidy, with no trim damage and no trace left in the cabin.

STEP 03

Handed over with the keys

Certification is issued on the day and the app is configured, so the customer can notify their insurer immediately and drive away already protected.

After handover we look after the customer directly — setup support, health checks and renewal reminders — so your team is not fielding questions about a product we installed. Where you would rather the security package read as your own, we can present the certificate and customer documentation in your dealership's name.

Trade rates

Agreed with your dealership directly

We do not publish trade rates, because the right arrangement depends on what you prepare, what you want to offer your customers and how you would rather present it. Some dealers pass enquiries to us and take a fee; others build the security package into the vehicle and present it as their own.

Either works. Tell us roughly how many vehicles you prepare each month and how you would like it to sit in your process, and we will come back to you with something that fits. There is no cost to set up, and we ask for a minimum of 3 vehicles per month.

If you would rather simply refer customers to us, that is covered on our referrals and trade rates page.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you fit trackers at dealership premises?
Yes. All of our installation is mobile, so we come to your showroom, workshop or compound. Vehicles never leave your site, which means your preparation schedule and delivery dates are not affected.
Will fitting a tracker delay a customer handover?
No. We fit during vehicle preparation rather than after sale, so the car is ready before the customer ever sees it. Trade work is given priority booking, so a delivery date is never held up waiting on us.
Do we have to hold stock or handle warranty?
No. We supply, fit, certify and support the system. Your team never holds stock, never handles a warranty claim and never has to explain a technical specification.
What do dealerships get out of offering vehicle security?
A margin line at handover that replaces income lost elsewhere in the finance office, a genuine differentiator against other dealers, fewer post-delivery theft calls, and a reason to be back in contact with the customer at renewal and at the end of their finance agreement.
Are the trackers insurance approved?
Yes. We are Thatcham-accredited and a Meta Trak approved installer, fitting insurance-approved S5 and S7 trackers, deadlock systems and immobilisers. Certification is issued on the day of installation so the customer can notify their insurer immediately.
What trade rates do you offer dealerships?
Trade rates are agreed directly with each dealership rather than published, because the right arrangement depends on volume and on what you want to offer your customers. Tell us roughly how many vehicles you prepare each month and we will come back to you.
Which areas do you cover for dealership work?
We work across London, Surrey and the Home Counties from our West London base in Holland Park, W11, and travel further by arrangement for regular trade partners.
Can you brand the handover for our dealership?
Yes. We can supply the security certificate and customer documentation presented in your dealership's name, so the security package reads as part of your own handover rather than a third-party add-on.
